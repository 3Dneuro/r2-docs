Good practice
=============

- Loosen the flex cable before you move the probe, the connector or the holder. Tension it again once the connector sits in a basket.
- Tighten the clamp screw from above only. It pushes the wedge clamp; it never touches the drive.
- Do transfers next to the stereotax, so the adapter goes straight from the case into the frame.
- For R2rails, use the removable window on the back of the case to reach the rail's screws without taking the drive out.
- Fasten the basket panel screw again every time you swivel the basket.
